# Upstream source research

| Project | Rendering/capture architecture | Optical and performance observations | License decision |
|---|---|---|---|

Continuous shared rAF supports springs/glints. Full-document/per-lens snapshots and continuous frames were unsuitable for this narrower performance target. | MIT, copyright Htet Lin Aung. Mutation debounce, unified cooldown, IntersectionObserver, dirty-only rAF, same-size `texSubImage2D`, conditional mipmaps. | Rounded-rect SDF, layered edge/rim/base displacement, in-shader Gaussian samples, ambient sampling, RGB split, tint, directional specular. | MIT, copyright Zaki. ## Resulting choices

The v0.1 implementation uses one raw WebGL2 viewport renderer, one shared periodically refreshed DOM texture, registered DOM surfaces, rounded-SDF lens displacement, tiered scattering/chromatic work, resource reuse, and a sleeping render loop. It does not copy unlicensed code, require consumers to render their page in WebGL, capture the entire document, or rasterize at 60 fps.
